The ideas/lifestyle.json file was NOT written, as the coordinator instructed.
Every URL below was copied verbatim from WebSearch results. None was constructed.
"sat" = number of returned apps.apple.com results that literally target the phrase in their title (0-10).
Observation: most niche hits have 2025-2026 IDs (id67xxxxxxxx-id68xxxxxxxx). These are new indie apps, often with few ratings.
So the niches are crowded but the competitors are weak, and they fill up fast.
