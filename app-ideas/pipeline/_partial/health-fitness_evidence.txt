HEALTH & FITNESS (health-fitness) — PARTIAL EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-09-30. 16 WebSearch calls made before the session-wide 200-search cap was hit.
All URLs below are copied verbatim from WebSearch results. No Apple APIs used.
"sat" = number of returned apps.apple.com results with the niche phrase in the TITLE.
General observation: in 2025-2026 many niches filled up with fresh indie apps (id67xxxxxxxx).
A niche that was empty in 2024 now often has 5-9 new, weak-looking English apps.
So the realistic edge is 50-language localization + Apple Watch/HealthKit polish, not being first.
