E. TAKEAWAYS
=====================================================================
- Obvious craft converters (cross stitch, fuse beads, PBN, string art, tattoo stencils, quilt design, paint mixing,
  hair formulas, seasonal color) were flooded by new apps in 2025–26 (many IDs 674x–680x). Do NOT target those head terms.
- Real openings found: stained glass pattern design, lithophane STL generation, brand-neutral laser engraving prep,
  and tesserae mosaic / Islamic geometric patterns.
- A good next step with fresh budget: verify the section D craft sub-niches with site:apps.apple.com queries, highest value first: needlepoint,
  sashiko, peyote/brick stitch, mosaic crochet, kumihimo, tablet weaving, EPP, icing color, polymer clay, laser box generator.
